Here are some common questions shareholders ask before beginning the recovery process.

Ask Us Directly →
Can shares transferred to IEPF be recovered? +

Shares transferred to IEPF may be claimed by the eligible claimant subject to applicable requirements, documentation and the prescribed process.

What if I have old physical share certificates? +

Old physical share certificates can still be relevant. The appropriate process depends on the company, shareholder records, current regulations and the documents available.

Can legal heirs claim shares of a deceased shareholder? +

In appropriate cases, legal heirs or other eligible claimants may be able to establish their entitlement through the applicable transmission or claim procedure.

Do you assist with unclaimed dividends? +

Yes. We provide assistance relating to unclaimed or unpaid dividends and help clients understand the relevant documentation and recovery process.

How do I know whether my case is recoverable? +

The best approach is to review the available shareholder and company records. You can contact us with the basic details of your case for an initial discussion.

Do you assist clients living outside India? +

Shareholders residing outside India may also require assistance with Indian shareholding, dividend and documentation matters. The specific requirements depend on the case.
